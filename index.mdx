## How It Works

Claude Web Builder uses a `CLAUDE.md` file to turn Claude Code into a guided web-building assistant. When you open the project, Claude reads those instructions and walks you through six phases automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clone and open the project">
    Clone the repo and run `claude` inside the project folder. Claude reads the instructions and greets you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer questions about your business">
    Claude asks about your business name, audience, visual style, and content in four short conversational rounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claude builds your page">
    After you approve the design direction, Claude scaffolds a Next.js project, writes all the code, and runs a QA pass — without asking for per-step approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview, iterate, and deploy">
    Review screenshots at `localhost:3000`, give feedback, and when you're happy, Claude deploys to Vercel and gives you a live URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Get

Every page Claude builds includes:

- **Custom design** — not a template. Colors, fonts, and layout chosen for your specific business.
- **Professional copy** — run through the humanizer skill to remove AI writing patterns.
- **Mobile-first layout** — tested at 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
- **SEO-ready** — meta tags, heading hierarchy, and structured data built in from the start.
- **Live URL** — deployed to Vercel's sandbox, no account needed. Shareable immediately.
